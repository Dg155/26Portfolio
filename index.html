About Me

I am Daniel Boghossian, a Computer Science and Game Development graduate with a passion for creating experiences. Ever since playing my first video game, Super Mario World on the Nintendo DS, I knew I wanted to pursue a career in game development. My goal is to provide others with the same joy, comfort, and lasting memories that video games have given me throughout my life.

In middle school, I taught myself how to program, sparking a fascination with the systems and mechanics that bring games to life. The ability to create something from nothing, a something that people around the world can experience and interact with, is an art form I wish to devote the rest of my life to.

Since writing my first lines of code, I have created over a dozen games across multiple engines and programming languages. Every day, I strive to learn something new, refine my skills, and expand my perspective as both a developer and a creator. I will make an impact on the world.
